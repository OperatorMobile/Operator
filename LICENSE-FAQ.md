# License FAQ

This is a plain-language summary, not a replacement for LICENSE.md.

## What license does Operator use?

Operator uses the Functional Source License, Version 1.1, ALv2 Future License.

## Can I read, modify, and use the code?

Yes, for permitted purposes under LICENSE.md. Internal use, learning, research,
and non-competing development are allowed.

## Can I publish a fork?

Yes, if the fork complies with LICENSE.md. Keep attribution and do not remove
required notices.

## Can I build a competing app or hosted service from this code?

No. The license prohibits making the software available to others in a
commercial product or service that substitutes for Operator or offers the same
or substantially similar functionality.

## Does the license become more permissive later?

Yes. Under the selected FSL variant, the software receives an Apache License
2.0 future license on the second anniversary of the date that version is made
available.
